
In Sections~\ref{sync_max:sec:decision_cd} and \ref{sync_max:sec:decision_cr}
we investigate the complexity of deciding complete distinguishability, complete reachability,
and sync-maximality. 
Also, we prove that the problem to decide whether two subsets are distinguishable is \PSPACE-complete.
In \cref{sync_max:decision:sec:binary-SI}, 
we investigate these problems, and the problem to decide synchronizablity,
for binary SI-automata as input,
which appears in a separate section as  
complete reachability, complete distinguishability, and sync-maximality
are equivalent properties for binary SI-automata by \cref{sync_max:prop:binary-SI}.
An overview of our results and those from the literature on complete reachability, complete distinguishability, and sync-maximality,
is given in \cref{tab:complexity}.
