
By the result of Ferens \& Szyku\l a~\cite{DBLP:conf/icalp/FerensS23} (see \cref{sync_max:thm:complete_reach_in_P})
complete reachability
can be decided in polynomial time.

With regard to the \NL-hardness results,
whose proofs are from~\cite{DBLP:conf/dlt/Hoffmann23}
with some minor corrections, we pose the \emph{open problem} whether
deciding complete reachability is in~\NL.
Szyku\l a \& Zyzik~\cite{SzykulaZyzik24} (personal communication)
informed me that they found proofs showing that
deciding complete distinguishability
and deciding sync-maximality are both \PSPACE-complete.
As noted in \cref{tab:complexity} the precise computational complexity
of deciding complete distinguishability for binary automata
is unknown, as well as the precise complexity for complete distinguishability/sync-maximality
for binary automata and for SI-automata as input.
