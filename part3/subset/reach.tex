This section contains only results from the literature and no new results.
\Cref{sync_max:sec:reach_k-sets} 
investigates automata in which all $k$-sets
are reachable for some $k \ge 1$.
We give a sufficient condition for synchronizability
in terms of reachability of $k$-sets
and state structural limitations
in $n$-state automata where all $(n-1)$-sets are reachable
or where all $(n - 2)$-set are reachable.
