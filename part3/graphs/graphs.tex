Here, we introduce digraphs
associated to automata and transformation semigroups 
that we use to deduce complete reachability and complete distinguishability.
Section~\ref{sync_max:sec:BVC-graphs},~\ref{sync_max:sec:augmentative}, and~\ref{sync_max:sec:rystsov_graph}
review existing constructions from the literature.
We introduce the Bondar-Casas-Volkov graphs, which apply to general automata, and the Rystsov graphs, which only apply to SI-automata,
from the literature.
\cref{sec:extended_Rystsov_graph} extends these graphs to arbitrary transformation semigroups.
Then we investigate the influence of the group of units
on the introduced digraphs (\cref{sync_max:graphs:sec:grpunits}),
relations between them (Section~\ref{sync_max:graphs:sec:relations}), give 
examples that highlight their differences (\cref{sync_max:graphs:sec:examples}), 
and finally formulate sufficient conditions in terms of connectedness notions to deduce
complete reachability, complete distinguishability, or
 lower bounds for the size of accepting automata of sets of synchronizing words (Section~\ref{sync_max:graphs:sec:cr_cd}).
 In \cref{sync_max:graphs:sec:cr_cd}, we also point out some
 relations to congruences and minimal automata.
