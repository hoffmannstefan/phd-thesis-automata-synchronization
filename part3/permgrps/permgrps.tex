Here, we investigate the properties of synchronizability, reachability, and distinguishability
in transformation monoids generated by a permutation group
and some non-permutation.
\cref{sec:prim_groups}
recaps some known characterizations for primitive groups, and gives new characterizations
in terms of minimality, synchronizability, complete reachability,
and complete distinguishability of associated automata. 
The subclass of synchronizing groups
is introduced in \cref{sync_max:perm:sec:sync_grps}. This section also discusses $k$-homogeneous groups.
\Cref{sec:A-primitive}
introduces the new notion of $A$-primitivity for a subset $A$
of the permutation domain as a generalization of primitivity.
We show that several properties of primitive groups carry over to $A$-primitive groups.
