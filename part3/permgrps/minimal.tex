Davies~\cite[Cor.~3.3]{DBLP:journals/mst/Davies18}
observed (see also~\cite[Cor.~4.2.4]{DBLP:phd/basesearch/Davies19}) 
that for a permutation automaton $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$, $\mathcal A_{q, F}$
is minimal for each $q \in Q$ and $\emptyset \ne F \subsetneq Q$
if and only if $\TM{\mathcal A}$ is a primitive permutation group.
Here, in \cref{sync_max:thm:prim_min}, 
we state and prove her result, also with a corresponding statement related
to morphisms.
Similarly as in \cref{syncmax:rem:square-brackets},
we use square brackets
to combine two related statements, one making
a statement on all  automata with $G \subseteq \TS{A}$ 
and the other on all  automata with $G = \TS{A}$.

\begin{theorem}[Davies~\cite{DBLP:journals/mst/Davies18,DBLP:phd/basesearch/Davies19}]
	\label{sync_max:thm:prim_min}
	Let $G$ be a permutation group on the finite set $Q$ 
	with $|Q| \ge 3$. Then the following are equivalent.	
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item \label{sync_max:appl:prim} $G$
		is a primitive permutation group.		
	
		\item \label{sync_max:appl:hom}
		For every $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$
		with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$ [with $G = \TS{\mathcal A}$]
		there exists no morphism $\varphi \colon Q \to S$
		into an automaton  $\semiautomat{B}{S}{\Sigma}{\mu}$
		such that $1 < |\applyfunc{Q}{\varphi}| < |Q|$.
				
		\item \label{sync_max:appl:min} 
		For every $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$
		with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$ [with $G = \TS{\mathcal A}$]
		the automaton $\mathcal A_{q, F}$ is minimal for all $F \subseteq Q$ with $F \notin \{\emptyset, Q\}$ and all $q \in Q$.		
	\end{enumerate}	
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$\ref{sync_max:appl:prim} \Rightarrow \ref{sync_max:appl:hom}$: 
As every $\TS{\mathcal A}$-congruence
is a $G$-congruence for automata $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$
with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$ (resp.~$G = \TS{\mathcal A}$),
primitivity of $G$
yields the absence of any non-trivial $\TS{\mathcal A}$-congruences
for all $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$
with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$ (resp.~$G = \TS{\mathcal A}$).
By \crefitem{prelim:thm:homtheorem}{prelim:thm:homtheorem:2},
the kernel relation of every morphism is trivial,
which gives~\cref{sync_max:appl:hom}.

$\ref{sync_max:appl:hom} \Rightarrow \ref{sync_max:appl:min}$: 
By \crefitem{prelim:thm:homtheorem}{prelim:thm:homtheorem:2},
all $\semiautomat{A}{Q}{\Sigma}{\delta}$
with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$ (resp.~$G = \TS{\mathcal A}$)
do not admit any non-trivial $\TS{\mathcal A}$-congruence.
Consider $\mathcal A$ with $G = \TS{\mathcal A}$.
Then $\mathcal A$ is a permutation automaton not admitting any non-trivial $\TM{\mathcal A}$-congruences.
Since in a permutation automaton a trap state is not reachable from any other state, from
\cref{prelim:cor:simpleaut} 
we obtain that $\mathcal A$ must be strongly connected,
which yields that $G$ is transitive.
Then, every $\mathcal A$ with $G \subseteq \TS{\mathcal A}$
is strongly connected. 
So, by \cref{prelim:cor:uniform-min}, from \cref{sync_max:appl:hom} we obtain \cref{sync_max:appl:min}.

$\ref{sync_max:appl:min} \Rightarrow \ref{sync_max:appl:prim}$: 
By assumption and  \cref{prelim:cor:uniform-min} every automaton $\mathcal A$
with $G = \TS{\mathcal A}$ does not admit any non-trivial $\TS{\mathcal A}$-congruence,
which is equivalent to primitivity of $G$.
\end{proof}

\crefitem{sync_max:thm:prim_min}{sync_max:appl:min} fails for $|Q| = 2$, 
as witnessed by the two-state automaton in which all letters induce self-loops. 
This automaton is not minimal for any choice of start state and subset of final states
and its transformation monoid is a primitive group.
Note that \crefitem{sync_max:thm:prim_min}{sync_max:appl:hom} does not exclude
the possibility of a non-trivial isomorphism.
