\cref{sycn_max:grp:Aprim:sec:intro} introduces $A$-primitive groups and states some basic properties.
In \cref{syncmax:sec:trans-abprim} we investigate orbits of $A$-primitive groups and introduce strongly $\{a,b\}$-primitive
groups. In \cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:distinguishability}, we collect some technical results
relating distinguishability, synchronizability, and orbit sizes to $\{a,b\}$-primitivity.
Then we show that the following known
characterizations of primitivity
admit natural generalizations to (strong) $\{a,b\}$-primitivity: Higman's orbital graph characterization (\cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:orbital}),
Jordan's characterization via generating sets for the symmetric group (\cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:sym_grp}),
and Rystsov's characterizations related to synchronizability properties (\cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:sync}).
Then, we give characterizations in terms of reachability
and distinguishability properties  (\cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:reach}).
Lastly, we note a characterizing property that resembles
$2$-homogeneity (\cref{sync_max:grp:Aprim:2hom})
and yields a new characterization of primitivity.
