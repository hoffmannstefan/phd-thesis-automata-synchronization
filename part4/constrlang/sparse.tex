 In \cref{constr_sync:subsec:sparse_NP}, 
we show that for sparse languages, the constrained synchronization problem is always in~\NP.
Then, in Section~\ref{sec:constr_sync:finite}, 
we show that for finite languages, which are sparse, the problem is in~$\AC^0$.
\Cref{constr_sync:subsec:sparse_L}
collects sufficient conditions for containment in \LOGSPACE. 
In \cref{constrsync:sec:letter-bounded:trichotomy}, we conclude with a full classification for the subclass of letter-bounded constraint languages, characterizing
precisely when the problem is in $\AC^0$, \LOGSPACE-complete or \NP-complete. %\todo{aussagen verlinken?}
Recall that by Proposition~\ref{constr_sync:thm:cf_bounded_equals_sparse} the sparse regular languages are precisely the 
bounded regular languages.
