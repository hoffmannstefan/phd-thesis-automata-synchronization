Here, we discuss the following classes of automata
that appear throughout the present work
and collect results of relevance to us:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item synchronizing automata (\cref{prelim:sec:sync_aut}) and synchronizing automata with a trap state (\SEC~\ref{prelim:sync:trap}),
	
	\item unary automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:ternarybinaryunary}),
				
	\item automata with one-point singular idempotents (OI-automata) and automata with simple idempotents (SI-automata) (\cref{prelim:subclasses:SI}),
	
	\item permutation-reset automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:permutation-reset}),
		
	\item monotonic automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:monotonic}),
	
	\item automata whose strongly connected components are blocks (\cref{prelim:subclasses:block}),
		
	\item partially ordered automata (\cref{prelim:sec:pto}), 
	
	\item $\rho$-automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:rho-automata}),
	
	\item commutative automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:commutative}),
	    
	\item solvable automata (\cref{prelim:subclasses:solvable}),
	 which generalize commutative and partially ordered automata.
\end{enumerate}

See \cref{prelim:classes:fig:inclusions} for a Hasse diagram of the inclusions that hold among these classes.

The following content is new and did not appear before in the literature:

\begin{enumerate}
\item the novel class of $\rho$-automata,

\item the results on automata whose strongly connected components are blocks,

\item the structural results on solvable automata and the result that a solvable automaton admitting
a word of rank $r$ has a word of rank at most 
$r$ and of length at most $n - r$ and 

\item new structural results on solvable automata.
\end{enumerate}

The remaining results presented are known and taken from the literature (maybe presented a little differently).

Other classes of automata, for example circular automata, completely
reachable, completely distinguishable or
sync-maximal automata are only used in Part~\ref{part:aut} and so not introduced
here but separately in Part~\ref{part:aut}.
