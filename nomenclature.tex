\label{chap:notationindex}

\noindent Standard logical and set-theoretical notation
that is briefly mentioned (and fixed) at the beginnings
of \cref{intro:sec:basic,sec:logic_set_theory_number_theory}
and common %well-known
expressions, as, for example, $\binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$, $u^n$ or $t^n$ for the $n$-fold concatenation product (resp.~composition) of a word $u$ or transformation $t$, etc.,
do not appear in the notation index.
We assume a sufficient level of familiarity
with such notions on the part of the reader to not feel it necessary
to include them in this index. Furthermore, notations that are only mentioned in a single paragraph (for example, in a discussion section)
and are not used beyond that are also not included.
